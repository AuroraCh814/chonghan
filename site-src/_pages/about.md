<p>I am a master’s student in <a href="https://amcs.upenn.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Applied Mathematics and Computational Science at the University of Pennsylvania</strong></a>. I am currently advised by <a href="https://nikolaimatni.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Prof. Nikolai Matni</strong></a>.</p>

<p>Previously, I received my B.S. in Mathematics of Computation from UCLA, where I was advised by <a href="https://chentangmark.github.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Prof. Chen Tang</strong></a> in the <a href="https://chentangmark.github.io/lab/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Trustworthy Autonomous Mobility Systems (TAMS) Lab</strong></a>.</p>

<p>During my undergraduate studies, I explored a range of topics, including financial mathematics, combinatorics, large language models, multimodal large language models, and vision-language models.</p>

<p>My primary interests are now robot learning, optimal control, and their intersection. My long-term goal is to build reliable, controllable, and safe robotic and autonomous systems for the real world.</p>
