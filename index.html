I’m interested in building reliable autonomous systems, with a focus on autonomous driving, safety-critical control, and robotics.

A little background

Previously, I earned my B.S. in Mathematics of Computation at UCLA. I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Chen Tang on CleanGuide, our work on adapting pretrained diffusion policies at deployment, as well as on mobile manipulation.

My mathematical background shapes how I approach research: understanding why a system works, when it can be trusted, and how to make it more reliable.
