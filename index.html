I am a master’s student in Applied Mathematics and Computational Science at the University of Pennsylvania. My research interests are learning-based control, generative robot policies, and reliable robotic decision-making.

Previously, I studied Mathematics of Computation at UCLA. I have been working with Prof. Chen Tang and Seunghun Oh on deployment-time adaptation of diffusion policies.

My background is in mathematics, optimal control, probability, and optimization. I am interested in how learned models and policies can adapt to new objectives while supporting safe and reliable decisions. My current work studies clean-action guidance for diffusion policies and execution-horizon selection in learned-model MPC.

Selected Publications

    Yomi Anifowoshe and Chong Han
    Ars Combinatoria, 167, 213–223, 2026
    HTML

    A study of adding–swapping insertion maps for higher-power permutations, using cycle-count signatures and local residue transitions to characterize the structure and limitations of these maps.
