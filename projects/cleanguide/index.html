CleanGuide

Clean-Action-Aligned Guidance for Deployment-Time Adaptation of Diffusion Policies

Work with Seunghun Oh and Chen Tang. Submitted to ICRA 2027.

Research question

How can a pretrained diffusion policy respond to a new deployment objective without retraining, using only forward evaluations of the deployment cost?

Approach

Deployment costs are defined by the rollout induced by a clean, executable action chunk. CleanGuide aligns the denoising target with this clean-action space. A schedule-aware local Gaussian proposal generates candidates efficiently, and their costs determine the guidance update.

Evaluation settings

The manuscript studies unseen-obstacle adaptation in 2D and 7-DoF Panda motion planning, alongside residual-reward adaptation in MuJoCo.

Research context

This project connects the behavioral prior of a pretrained generative policy to objectives introduced at execution time.
